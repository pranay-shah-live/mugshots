Privacy Policy

How we protect your personal information and uploaded media.

This Privacy Policy describes how Mugshots, owned and operated by PRANAY HIMANSHU SHAH ("Merchant", "we", "us", or "our"), collects, utilizes, and protects your personal information when you visit or purchase products from our website.

1. Information We Collect

When you place an order or contact us, we collect:

  • Order & Contact Data: Full Name, phone number, WhatsApp contact, email address, postal delivery address, and pincode.
  • Customization Media & Content: Personal photographs, images, quotes, or corporate logos transmitted to our team via WhatsApp or email for custom printing.
  • Transaction Identifiers: Razorpay payment reference IDs and order totals. We do NOT store or have access to your credit/debit card numbers, UPI PINs, or banking passwords.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly for:

  • Generating customized ceramic merchandise according to your specifications.
  • Facilitating physical package delivery with our licensed courier partners.
  • Communicating with you regarding order confirmations, digital design previews, and tracking notifications.
  • Compliance with legal, taxation, and payment processing regulations.

3. Confidentiality of Custom Photographs

Your personal memories are sacred. We treat all personal photographs and private messages shared for mug printing with the highest level of confidentiality. We will never publish, share, distribute, or use your submitted personal photos in marketing materials or public galleries without your explicit prior written consent. Once an order has been successfully fulfilled and the return window has elapsed, temporary print-staging files are permanently deleted.

4. Secure Payment Processing (Razorpay)

Online payments on this store are processed through Razorpay, a RBI-authorized, PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant payment aggregator. During checkout:

  • Payment transactions are encrypted using end-to-end 256-bit SSL protocols.
  • Your payment data is collected directly by Razorpay and is subject to Razorpay’s Privacy Policy.
  • You agree to share information entered on this page with PRANAY HIMANSHU SHAH and Razorpay, adhering to applicable Indian laws.

5. Disclosure to Third Parties

We only share necessary shipping data (your name, address, and phone number) with trusted logistics partners (e.g. BlueDart, Delhivery, DTDC) exclusively to fulfill shipment delivery. We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third-party advertising brokers.

6. Security Measures

We maintain reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse in compliance with the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011.
