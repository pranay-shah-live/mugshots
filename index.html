Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about placing customized orders with Mugshots.

How do I send my photos and customization text?
As soon as you complete checkout, you will receive an Order ID on screen. Simply click the "Send Photos on WhatsApp" button, or email your photos to Pranayshah995@gmail.com quoting your Order ID. Our design team verifies photo quality before printing!
How long does delivery take?
Customization and printing usually take 1 to 2 business days. Courier shipping across India takes an additional 4 to 6 business days depending on your delivery pincode. You will receive tracking details via SMS/WhatsApp once dispatched.
Are the mugs microwave and dishwasher safe?
Yes! Our Classic ceramic mugs are 100% microwave safe and dishwasher safe. For Magic color-changing mugs, gentle hand washing is recommended to preserve the heat-sensitive thermochromic coating over years of use.
What is your return or cancellation policy?
Because each item is customized specifically with your personal photos or name, orders cannot be returned or cancelled once printing has commenced. However, in the rare event of transit damage or manufacturing defect, report it to us with photos within 48 hours of delivery, and we will dispatch an immediate free replacement!
