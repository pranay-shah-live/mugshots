# Mugshots - Customized Mugs & Merch Online Store

A lightweight, high-performance static eCommerce storefront built for **Mugshots**—an online custom mugs and personalized gifting brand owned and operated by **PRANAY HIMANSHU SHAH**.

## Key Features
- **Offline Customization Architecture:** Customers select items and pay securely online via Razorpay. Post-checkout, they are seamlessly guided to share high-resolution photos and text directly over WhatsApp or email quoting their unique Order ID.
- **Razorpay Standard Checkout Integration:** Integrated with Razorpay `checkout.js`, accepting UPI, Credit/Debit Cards, and Netbanking.
- **100% Payment Gateway KYC Compliance:** Fully compliant with Razorpay KYC standards for Indian merchants, including dedicated pages for:
  - About Us (`about.html`)
  - Contact Us (`contact.html`) with direct hotline, email, and operating location
  - Terms & Conditions (`terms.html`) with Mumbai jurisdiction
  - Cancellation & Refund Policy (`refund-policy.html`) covering customized goods and 48-hour damage replacement
  - Shipping & Delivery Policy (`shipping-policy.html`) with pan-India timelines
  - Privacy Policy (`privacy-policy.html`)
- **Ready for GitHub Pages:** Pure static HTML5, CSS3, and modern vanilla JavaScript with zero build dependencies and `.nojekyll` configuration.
