About Mugshots

Bespoke custom merchandise handcrafted to celebrate life's most meaningful moments.

Who We Are

Mugshots is a specialized online personalized gifting and customized merchandise studio owned and operated by PRANAY HIMANSHU SHAH based in Mumbai, India. We believe that everyday essentials—like your morning coffee mug—should carry personal warmth, joy, and memories.

Our Philosophy: Thoughtful Customization

Unlike automated mass-printers that automatically print low-resolution or misaligned images, Mugshots blends online ordering convenience with dedicated offline graphic design support. Every photo and text snippet you submit is manually reviewed, color-balanced, and test-fitted by our design team before printing on our high-temperature sublimation heat presses.

Materials & Quality Standards

  • Grade-A Ceramic: High-density, lead-free, non-toxic ceramic that retains temperature and withstands daily microwave and dishwasher usage.
  • 300+ DPI Sublimation Infusion: We use specialized heat-activated dyes that permeate the ceramic glaze directly, ensuring your prints never peel, chip, or fade.
  • Protective Drop-Proof Packaging: Every mug is cushioned inside custom-molded thermocol and thick corrugated outer cartons to withstand transit across India.
