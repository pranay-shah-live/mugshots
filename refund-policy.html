Cancellation & Refund Policy

Clear, transparent guidelines for customized and personalized merchandise.

Important Summary: Customized products are non-returnable and non-refundable once printing has commenced, except for items that arrive damaged, defective, or incorrect. Please contact us within 48 hours of delivery with unboxing photos/video for immediate assistance.

1. Cancellation Policy

  • Before Customization/Printing: If you wish to cancel an order, you must notify us via WhatsApp at +91 8169718315 or email support@mugshots.com within 2 hours of placing the order. If production or printing has not yet started, we will cancel the order and issue a full refund.
  • After Customization/Printing Begins: Once our workshop has begun printing your custom photo or text onto the ceramic or merchandise, the order cannot be cancelled, as the product is customized specifically for you and cannot be re-stocked or resold.

2. Damaged, Defective, or Incorrect Items

Every Mugshots product is carefully inspected and packaged in shock-resistant packaging before dispatch. However, in the unfortunate event that your ceramic mug or item arrives cracked, chipped, broken, or with a substantial printing defect:

  • Please notify us within 48 hours of delivery.
  • Contact us via WhatsApp at +91 8169718315 or email support@mugshots.com.
  • Include your Order ID, a clear photo of the shipping label on the package, and clear photographs/video showing the broken or defective portion.

3. Resolution: Free Replacement or Refund

Upon prompt verification of transit damage or defect:

  • Free Express Replacement (Recommended): We will fast-track a brand new customized replacement at zero additional shipping or product cost to you.
  • Refund Option: If you prefer a refund rather than a replacement, we will initiate a full refund of the purchase price to your original payment method.

4. Refund Processing Timelines

  • Refunds are processed automatically through our payment gateway (Razorpay).
  • Once initiated, the refunded amount typically reflects in your bank account, card statement, or UPI account within 5 to 7 business days, depending on your issuing bank's settlement cycle.

5. Non-Qualifying Scenarios

Refunds or replacements cannot be issued in the following scenarios:

  • Customer submitted low-resolution, blurred, or dark photos despite confirmation.
  • Customer provided an incorrect or incomplete delivery address or wrong contact number leading to failed courier delivery attempts.
  • Damage reported beyond the mandatory 48-hour post-delivery window.
  • Minor variations in color hue between digital smartphone screens (RGB) and ceramic dye sublimation prints (CMYK).
