Shipping & Delivery Policy

Transparent information regarding dispatch timelines and pan-India shipping.

1. Shipping Coverage

Mugshots (operated by PRANAY HIMANSHU SHAH) ships customized mugs and merchandise to serviceable postal pincodes throughout India. We partner with reliable domestic express couriers including Delhivery, Blue Dart, DTDC, and Speed Post.

2. Order Processing & Production Timeline

  • Customization & Design Verification: Once you complete checkout and share your photos/text on WhatsApp or email, our graphic team prepares your digital proof within 24 hours.
  • Printing & Quality Inspection: Sublimation printing, cooling, and visual inspection typically take 1 to 2 business days.
  • Total time from order placement to courier dispatch is generally 2 to 3 business days.

3. Delivery Timelines (Pan-India)

Metro Cities (Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Chennai, Kolkata, Pune, Hyderabad) 3 to 5 business days post dispatch
Tier 2 & Tier 3 Cities / Rest of India 5 to 7 business days post dispatch
Special Remote Regions (Northeast, J&K, Island territories) 7 to 10 business days post dispatch

4. Shipping Charges

Standard shipping costs are transparently detailed during checkout or incorporated into promotional product bundles. Unless specified otherwise during promotional periods, standard flat shipping across India is free on orders above ₹399.

5. Tracking Your Shipment

As soon as your package is handed over to our courier partner, you will receive an automated tracking link and AWB number on your registered WhatsApp phone number and email address. You can track your parcel's real-time transit status at any time.

6. Safe Packaging Guarantee

Ceramic goods are fragile, which is why we take extra protective precautions. All mugs are enveloped in thick expanded polystyrene (thermocol) molds and packed inside 5-ply reinforced cardboard boxes.
